# Fresh initial-answer confirmation

**Complete:** both behavioral gates pass; no primary intervention passes selective-removal criteria, and no primary internal forecast addition passes its corrected test. Read the [complete report](reports/Initial_Answer_Confirmation_Report.md).

This is a separate, prospectively frozen follow-up to the completed shared-history stage. Its protocol and 32 fresh cases were fixed before target inference. The earlier stage's results motivated the context choice and are not fresh confirmation evidence for this stage.

- `PROTOCOL.md` and `data/manifest.json`: questions, design, gates and file hashes.
- `run.py`: context-matched directions, fresh activation extraction, generation, all fixed interventions and post-edit readouts.
- `predict.py`: 14-family fitting,12-family development selection, 26-family refit, frozen predictions for 32 new families; no new outcomes enter fitting.
- `judge.py` and `prepare_judging.py`: content-addressed blinded scoring with two pinned judges.
- `analyze.py`: complete-roster verification, paired intervals, family/domain analyses, multiplicity correction and declared gates.
- `report.py` and `figures.py`: complete results, including every condition and failed gate.
- `results/`: raw outputs, activations, predictions, judgments, readouts and analysis.
- `reports/Initial_Answer_Confirmation_Report.md`: complete stage report after analysis.
- `figures/`: PNG, PDF and SVG exports after analysis.

There are 29 conditions: 3 untreated references and 26 active or technical-control conditions. Every condition covers 32 fresh initial-answer families and 108 retained checks. Untreated retention outputs are exact reuses; edited retention outputs are newly generated. All new behavioral questions contain no prior assistant response.

The completed parent study remains intact. This extension is not a replacement analysis, a new training replicate, or a test of durable weight remediation. It has no observed-behavior forecast baseline. All source revisions and deviations are retained; no additional context, layer or dose search is permitted after its outcomes.
