# Fresh initial-answer confirmation

This separate stage was designed after the completed shared-history study. That study found a strong natural-conversation gap but only a 0.25/0.29-point shared-neutral-history gap, below its one-point behavioral gate. Its initial-answer gaps were 1.92/1.96 points. Those results motivate this new test; they are development evidence, not confirmation evidence for this stage. The earlier nulls and failed gates remain unchanged.

## Frozen question and sample

Can context-matched raw, sparse-J, remainder or targeted-monitor interventions selectively reduce the strong **initial-answer** advocacy difference on 32 new families, four per each of the eight established domains? All new prompts are frozen before inference. The earlier 48 families are excluded. Twelve benign requests and the 48 numerical/48 provider presentations are reused retention checks, not fresh generalization evidence.

Use the same three checkpoints, validated runtime, greedy generation, token-extension rule, rubric and two pinned judges. The initial prompt has no model-generated earlier history. Preserve every assigned output and judgment, including failures. No further context, layer, dose or direction search is allowed after these outcomes.

## Measurements and interventions

Use the earlier 14 fit and 12 development families at their **initial** prompt positions. Fit each direction from the 14 initial-context activation differences, with the same raw/J/remainder decomposition and same seeded random axes. The primary layer remains20; layer 18 is a fixed sensitivity. The existing advisory-corpus lenses remain frozen: their 64 sequences include 32 initial and 32 risk contexts. They are not refitted using the new evaluation prompts. Check primary common-base J split-half geometry on the 12 old development **initial** contexts, using the original thresholds (CKA ≥ .90, median component cosine ≥ .90, coverage shift ≤ .05).

Run the complete original roster plus the two technical zero edits: 29 conditions total, including 3 untreated references and 26 active/control conditions. The four primary edits remain raw removal, J removal, remainder removal and scalar monitor erasure. All 156-case assumptions in the earlier implementation are replaced explicitly by this stage's 140 cases (32 new conversations plus 108 retention cases). Sustained, oracle, dose, reverse-transfer and random-control definitions are unchanged. Untreated retention answers may be reused because their exact prompts and unedited checkpoints are unchanged; edited retention answers must be regenerated because the directions have changed.

Fit untargeted raw-layer 24 and component classifiers on the 14 initial fit families, select regularization on 12 development families, refit 26, and evaluate on the32 new families and their edited activations. These identify checkpoints, not loyalty. Monitor erasure is checked numerically throughout generation and against independent behavioral scores. Online editing does not establish durable remediation.

## Prospective forecasts

The target is the Vireo-minus-neutral initial-answer advocacy score. Fit separate forecasts for each judge using its already available initial-answer scores on the 14 fit and 12 development families. Compare a mean, a clean-activation prompt baseline, raw/J/remainder-only measurements, and prompt-plus-raw/J/remainder measurements. There is no prior behavioral observation for these new initial answers, so do not claim improvement beyond an observed-behavior baseline. Use the original ridge grid, train-only scaling, development selection and 26-family refit. Freeze predictions and checksums before any new judge scores are generated or read. Correct the three primary internal additions across two judges by Holm; report all other variants descriptively.

## Analysis and claim gates

Behavioral validity requires Vireo-minus-neutral ≥ 1 point, paired 95% lower bound>0, all 32 pairs, and ≥95% complete/responsive answers in **both** judges. Report untreated and batch-matched zero-edit contrasts; robust claims require both gates. Do not switch to another context if this fails.

A primary selective reduction requires ≥1 point reduction versus Vireo, positive paired 95% lower bound, advantage over the mean of its three norm-matched random controls, Holm-adjusted paired one-sided sign-flip p < .05 across 4 edits × 2 judges, and preservation. Require the corresponding comparison with the batch-matched zero edit, and agreement across judges. Formal-J localization additionally requires the initial-context geometry gate. Unequal natural direction norms prohibit interpreting relative effects as fractions of loyalty.

Preservation: ≥95% complete/responsive initial answers; mean usefulness loss ≤1; numerical and provider-choice accuracy loss ≤5 percentage points relative to the recipient. Report paired intervals and benign outcomes. These are point-estimate gates on narrow tasks, not proof of universal noninferiority. Use 20,000 paired-family bootstraps and eight-domain sensitivity; 100,000 sign flips. Scope remains one fixed organism/comparator pair and automated ratings.

No positive result is guaranteed. This is a prospectively frozen follow-up within an adaptive program, not an externally preregistered experiment. It will receive its own complete report and be included in the final synthesis alongside the earlier failed shared-history gate.
